# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our Pledge / 我们的承诺

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

我们作为社区成员、贡献者和领导者，承诺让每个人都能在无骚扰的环境中参与我们的社区，无论其年龄、体型、残疾状况、种族、性别特征、性别认同和表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向。

## Our Standards / 行为标准

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment / 有助于营造积极环境的行为:

* Being respectful of differing viewpoints / 尊重不同观点
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback / 给予并优雅地接受建设性反馈
* Focusing on what is best for the community / 以社区利益为重
* Showing empathy towards other community members / 对其他社区成员表示同理心

Examples of unacceptable behavior / 不可接受的行为:

* Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments / 挑衅、侮辱或贬低性评论
* Public or private harassment / 公开或私下骚扰
* Publishing others' private information without permission / 未经许可发布他人隐私信息
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate / 其他可能被认为不当的行为

## Enforcement / 执行

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team at security@planckbaka.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

滥用、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为可通过 security@planckbaka.com 向项目团队举报。所有投诉都将得到及时、公平的审查和处理。
